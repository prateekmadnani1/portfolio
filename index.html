About

I started out as a software engineer in Bengaluru, building Java services and cloud microservices at Checkpoint Systems and Blackhawk Network. Over time the questions I cared about shifted from how to build something to what we should build, who it is for, and how we would know it worked. That pulled me into product.

Today I lead platform modernization at AgingOptions in Seattle. Before that I owned AI and platform products at the American Arbitration Association and Dentite, and earned an MS in Management Information Systems at the University at Buffalo. My engineering background is what I lean on most: I can sit in an architecture review in the morning and an executive roadmap meeting in the afternoon, and translate between the two.

Most at home where AI, platforms and regulated industries meet: legal, healthcare and payments.

Keeping a 30-pages-a-day reading habit. Fifteen books in the first year.

Business case study

In-N-Out Burger: why a company that refuses to grow fast keeps winning

Independent case study · 2026

In-N-Out breaks most of the rules a fast-food chain is supposed to follow, and it is still one of the most loved brands in the category. I wanted to understand why. This case study traces where the model came from, then follows how the company's three core values, quality, people and control, turn into concrete strategy.

From there it looks at what that strategy produces economically, where it leaves the business exposed, and which lessons carry over to companies that will never sell a burger.
